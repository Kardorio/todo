Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - First stable release of Todo Manager for Factorio 2.1.
    - Shared tasks, subtasks, statuses, priorities and notes stored in the save.
    - Compact reminder with personal pins and display preferences for each player.
    - Saved locations with a camera preview and map navigation.
    - Production goals with selective ingredient additions as subtasks.
    - Alternative recipe selection and an unresearched recipe filter.
    - Task creation from hovered prototypes with configurable Ctrl + Shift + left-click.
    - English and French interfaces and translations.
  Changes:
    - Preserved tasks and preferences from development versions.
